Direction

Most plastic ID card printers only take CR80 (some also CR79). Print this size on paper or card stock with an office printer, then use a badge holder.

Select an element on the card or in Layers to edit it. Use the Add icons at the left of the card to place new elements. When the design is ready, save it and choose it in Card Studio under ⚙ Settings → Design Mapping.
