Create your first list

A list is one group of cards — for example Students, Staff, Substitutes, or Volunteers. Each list has its own spreadsheet, card design, and photo folder. You can create as many as you need.

How to use Card Studio

  1. The cards always match the list. Search or pick a filter (All, Missing photo, a grade) and the cards on the right update at once.
  2. Class: if your design has a Class / Section field (or Homeroom), a Class box lets you show one class, e.g. 12A — handy for calling students in during photo day. Choose a grade first to see only that grade’s classes.
  3. Click a name or a card to open it large. Use ‹ › (or the arrow keys) to move through the list, and ← Back (or Esc) to return.
  4. Print always prints what you see: the cards shown, or the one open card. If you tick checkboxes, it prints only the ticked cards.
  5. 📷 Photograph these … takes photos of everyone shown, one after another. Pick “Missing photo” first to photograph only people without one. With a card open, the same button says Take Photo. In the camera: Space captures, Enter saves.
  6. Lists: your lists are the tabs at the top. Click a tab to switch, ✎ next to the open tab to rename or remove it, and ＋ New List to add one. Each list has its own spreadsheet, design, and photo folder.
  7. Settings changes the spreadsheet, design, columns, or photo folder. Edits made in the spreadsheet load automatically when you come back to this tab.
